# Linux Release Readiness Lab charter

- Status: accepted
- Phase: validation
- Started: 2026-08-07

## Mission

Earn the first lawful dollar by reducing a real maintainer's Linux release work, then grow only when cleared revenue and customer evidence justify the next expense.

## Buyer

Primary buyers are commercial open-source maintainers, independent developer-tool vendors, and small teams that ship a supported or open-core Linux CLI. Unfunded volunteer maintainers are beneficiaries and potential referrers, not the primary revenue source.

## Problem

Generic repository health and security scores are already widely available. The Lab focuses on a concrete operational outcome:

- consistent versioning and release notes;
- automated Linux amd64/arm64 artifacts;
- checksums and software-bill-of-material signals;
- provenance/attestation signals where appropriate;
- package and license metadata;
- tested installation and uninstall instructions;
- maintainable release CI.

## Product boundary

At launch:

- public Go and Python CLI repositories only;
- static evidence collection that invokes no network client, subprocess, Git command, or target code; storage locality is not verified;
- the current collector produces prominently labeled, unverified synthetic or trusted-local demonstrations only;
- public repository evidence remains blocked until a disposable project-controlled acquisition environment verifies the upstream commit without trusting target Git metadata or executing target code;
- paid scope agreed before work and limited to a single repair outcome, delivered as a patch branch by default;
- an upstream pull request is opened only when invited and contribution policy permits it;
- upstream maintainer approval and contribution rules control any patch;
- no private code, production access, credentials, pentesting, vulnerability research, legal compliance, certification, or uptime promise.

## Decision rights

The AI chooses sample targets, checker design, report format, pricing experiments, public educational content, channel tests, and pivots. The human participates only when identity, service terms, a merchant relationship, taxes, money movement, credentials, or a customer contract requires a person.

## Economics

- Launch cash spend: $0.
- No XLM funding or payment route in validation.
- Use free local tooling and, after the publication gate, free repository/Page hosting.
- Do not purchase a domain until the written validation gate is met.
- Do not recommend a ChatGPT plan/API upgrade until cleared project revenue can cover its full recurring cost plus reserves for at least two billing periods.
- Complete applicable legal, tax, trade-name/entity, privacy, merchant, and bookkeeping checks before accepting any payment; afterward reserve receipts for documented taxes, fees, refunds, chargebacks, and delivery obligations.

## Stop or pivot

After ten samples and 100 qualified page views from relevant channels, the offer must produce at least five maintainer opt-ins and one explicit nonbinding request for a paid pilot. If not, change the buyer, outcome, channel, or price before building more infrastructure.

After three completed paid repairs, compare actual delivery time, rework, support burden, gross margin, and maintainer satisfaction. Automate only repeated evidence; preserve judgment in scoping and patch review.
