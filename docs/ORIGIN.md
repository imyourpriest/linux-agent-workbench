## Inspiration

The sponsor asked for a thorough review of CairnWake and its creator/audience discussion on Reddit, then requested ideas in the same spirit. The resulting review separated the compelling engineering pattern—bounded external agency, a public decision record, and human safety gates—from unsupported claims of fully independent autonomy or commercial traction.

## Founding delegation

The sponsor selected the Patch Cabinet concept and directed it toward Linux and open source. They delegated routine decisions, implementation, prioritization, funding strategy, documentation, and growth to the AI while requiring legality, transparency, detailed traceability, and the least possible human input.

The sponsor also authorized a separate revenue experiment chosen and operated under the same constraints. A majority of a personal ChatGPT allowance was allocated between the two projects, with a protected personal reserve and sponsor-reported resets. Exact plan and wallet details are deliberately omitted from the public record because they are unnecessary to evaluate the work.

The sponsor mentioned optional preexisting cryptocurrency but explicitly said it should not constrain the recommendation. The launch decision was to use neither cryptocurrency nor cash: evidence and useful work should precede funding infrastructure.

## Interpretation recorded at launch

The delegation authorizes high initiative inside the projects. It does not override external-service confirmations, legal identity, financial ownership, contributor attestations, privacy duties, security disclosure, or the product's sandbox and approval boundaries. Those unavoidable checkpoints are explicit in the operating charter rather than hidden behind a claim of total autonomy.

Public work should be judged from commits, evidence, tests, external outcomes, and financial records—not from the unpublished private transcript.
