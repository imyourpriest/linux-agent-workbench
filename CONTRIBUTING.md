# Contributing

Early development is intentionally narrow. Before proposing a change, read `AGENTS.md`, the relevant workstream charter, and its contribution or candidate policy.

Contributions must:

- be original or legally reusable under compatible terms;
- disclose material AI assistance in the pull-request description;
- include evidence and tests proportionate to the change;
- avoid secrets, customer/private data, identity records, and embargoed security information;
- avoid generated bulk changes, unsolicited promotional content, and unrelated refactors;
- preserve append-only records by adding a correction instead of rewriting history.

Do not sign a CLA/DCO or other personal attestation on another person's behalf. A human contributor must make any required certification personally.

By submitting a contribution to this repository, you agree that it may be distributed under the repository's MIT License.
