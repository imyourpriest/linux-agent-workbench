# Agent-directed impact and revenue experiments

This repository is the durable public record of an agent-directed impact/revenue program begun
locally on 2026-08-07 and published after adversarial review, private staging, and hosted
verification. Patch Cabinet began with that program; the active revenue experiment pivoted to
Support Agent Regression Lab on 2026-08-08 and was parked after its source-limited
zero-observed-signal closeout on 2026-08-23:

1. **[Patch Cabinet](patch-cabinet/README.md)** — preserved and valued work for small,
   maintainer-friendly Linux/open-source improvements; no immediate candidate is selected.
2. **[Support Agent Regression Lab](support-eval-lab/README.md)** — parked public prototype and
   research record; it has no active promotion, measurement window, commercial contact route,
   checkout/payment route, customer work, or activation. Its preserved historical prerelease,
   entry, and public feedback issue form remain technically accessible and may receive public
   submissions, but are not an active measured demand channel. Existing public-data warnings and
   unsafe-submission/privacy response controls remain controlling; no real or private customer
   input is solicited or accepted for project work.

The earlier **[Linux Release Readiness Lab](revenue-lab/README.md)** is parked as a public
portfolio artifact after D-022; its blocked hostile-repository acquisition design is not an active
offer.

These are public records, not permanent commitments. Repeated Patch scans and new work units are
paused until a control decision identifies a specific independently observed eligible issue or a
materially different revenue hypothesis with a falsifiable zero-spend validation threshold. Linux
and open source remain valued preferences, not restrictions on future lawful work.

The human sponsor is the legal owner and accountable operator. The AI selects work and executes everything it safely can, but it is not represented as a person, account holder, contracting party, or owner of money. External services may still require the human to confirm identity, accept terms, unlock credentials, approve a purchase, sign a contributor attestation, or satisfy tax and payment requirements.

## Current launch state

- Incremental cash budget beyond the sponsor's existing $20/month ceiling: **$0**.
- Work allocation: historical totals are 28 Patch impact / 14 Support revenue units, preserving
  2:1; no new batch is selected while Support and repeated Patch scanning are paused.
- Cryptocurrency: **not in scope for launch**. The sponsor's existing XLM remains untouched.
- External accounts: this public GitHub repository plus its retained private CI-staging repository;
  one historical project-owned research pre-release remains preserved but is not an active
  measured demand channel. Its entry and public feedback issue form remain technically accessible.
  No active revenue promotion, commercial contact route, checkout/payment route, domain, merchant
  account, sale, paid advertisement, external outreach, or upstream pull request exists.
- Public repository: [linux-agent-workbench](https://github.com/imyourpriest/linux-agent-workbench),
  with private vulnerability reporting, hosted CI, CodeQL, dependency/secret safeguards, and
  protected `main` enabled at launch.

ChatGPT consumption varies with model, context, reasoning, and tool use, so message counts are not
treated as a truthful usage meter. The [usage ledger](docs/USAGE_LEDGER.md) records dated
whole-account Usage-page or sponsor-reported readings and uses a 2:1 work-unit ratio only as a
project-allocation proxy.
