# AI Contribution Policy Starter v0.1.0 research prerelease

Proposed, unactivated draft for a free synthetic starter. It is not legal advice, compliance or
enforcement certification, identity verification, an AI-use detector, or proof of policy adoption.
It accepts no private input and offers no checkout or payment. The archive fingerprint is provided
for byte-consistency checking; SHA-256 is not a signature, attestation, or external trust.

If separately activated after all gates, feedback would use the project-owned privacy-minimal issue
form. Do not include names, email, phone, employer, private URLs, private repositories, secrets,
security reports, payment information, or other personal/private data.

If later activated, the final 14-day evaluation binds the exact GitHub release ID, tag, asset, and
feedback-form route/window. Success requires all of 10 downloads, three substantive issues from
distinct public accounts, and two intended adoption/use reports. Fewer than five downloads or zero
substantive feedback is failure; other completed outcomes are inconclusive. Downloads are not
unique people. All results keep payment disabled absent a separate future decision.
