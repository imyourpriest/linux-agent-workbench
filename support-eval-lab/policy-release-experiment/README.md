# Policy starter release experiment (prepared, not activated)

This directory contains inert drafts for a possible GitHub prerelease and privacy-minimal public
feedback issue form. Nothing here activates a release, form, channel, listing, payment route, or
contact. Demand, adoption, sales, customers, and revenue remain unvalidated; revenue is `$0.00`.

The earliest timestamp is only one gate. A future control-task decision and every evidence and
review condition in `ACTIVATION_CONTRACT.json` are also mandatory. August 24 activation is unsafe.
The validator is offline and never converts wall-clock time into authorization.

At the final exact 14-day checkpoint, success requires all three gates: the exact bound GitHub
release asset's REST `download_count` is at least 10, at least three substantive feedback issues
come through the exact activated form route/window from distinct public accounts, and at least two
reports state intended adoption or use. Failure is `download_count < 5` or zero substantive
feedback. Every other completed result is inconclusive. Failure or inconclusive means no payment,
revise or retire, and no second channel in this test. Success also leaves payment disabled pending
a future decision. Downloads are not unique people; issue aggregates retain no usernames or links.
