# Scope and boundaries

Included: a synthetic declaration, decision matrix, cautious contribution snippet, issue and pull
request checklist, ten-point evidence-linked audit, and asynchronous handoff template.

Prohibited: private input, credentials, identity or authority claims, legal advice, copyright
clearance, AI-use detection, compliance or certification claims, enforcement guarantees, customer
contact, payment, checkout, listing, activation, production access, or repository mutation.

Human maintainers remain accountable for policy, review, licensing, security reporting, and every
merge or rejection decision. The pack does not decide whether a contribution is acceptable.
