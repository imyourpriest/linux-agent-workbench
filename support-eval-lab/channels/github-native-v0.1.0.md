# GitHub-native discovery entry - v0.1.0

This is the GitHub-native experiment entry page. The free, AI-assisted research prototype helps
small B2B SaaS teams inspect ten original synthetic support-agent cases before and after a change.
It is an offline example, not a benchmark, certification, production gate, or active service. The
page is public and discoverable; its presence does not prove that a visitor came from the release.
