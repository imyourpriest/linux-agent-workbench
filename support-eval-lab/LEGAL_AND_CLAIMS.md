# Legal, privacy, and claims boundary

- Status: pre-sale draft; not terms of sale
- Updated: 2026-08-08

## Current prototype

All included cases, policies, organizations, conversations, and runs are fictional and project-
owned. `synthetic_mock` means a hand-authored demonstration, not a model benchmark. Reports must
not be represented as independent testing of a real system.

The checker performs bounded exact-string/action/citation assertions plus records human-review labels. It does
not certify truth, safety, fairness, accessibility, compliance, production readiness, or customer
satisfaction. "No regression in this sample" never means "no regression exists."

Human-review labels and reviewer competence are operator assertions, not authenticated facts. An
assertion pass can still be semantically wrong, negated, misleading, or incomplete; the rubric's
meaning/context review is required before any operator sign-off.

`compare-local` is a buyer-side local workflow, not a request to transfer input to this project.
It omits response text from reports and rejects several obvious sensitive-data patterns, but it is
not a complete privacy or data-loss-prevention scanner. Buyers must not send run JSONL files to the
project; a future custom pilot receives only the approved public policy source and a response-free
comparison report.

Response hashes are stable identifiers, not encryption or a confidentiality guarantee. Short,
common, or otherwise predictable responses may be recoverable by guessing and hashing candidates.

## Before any custom work

- Use only a customer-owned or authorized policy source that is already public and explicitly
  approved for the pilot. Any nonpublic or sanitized customer brief requires a future governed
  private-input, contract, retention, and deletion route and is not accepted at launch.
- Do not request or accept real transcripts, personal data, credentials, production access,
  regulated-domain content, confidential incident details, or third-party material without rights.
- Record ownership, permitted use, delivery license, retention/deletion, confidentiality, support,
  acceptance, cancellation, refund, and liability terms before payment.
- If public case-study permission is granted, revocation stops future first-party promotional use;
  already-public Git history, forks, caches, archives, and third-party copies may persist.
- The owner must complete applicable identity, tax, trade-name/entity, consumer-protection,
  merchant, bookkeeping, and sanctions/export checks. This draft is not legal or tax advice.

No customer input, contract, payment, merchant account, or sale exists today.
