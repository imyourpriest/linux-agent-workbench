# Validation plan

- Status: pre-channel
- Updated: 2026-08-08

## Why this test exists

Upwork's 2026 completed-job analysis reports 109% year-over-year growth for skills explicitly
referencing AI, 71% growth for AI chatbot development, and lists experimentation/testing among its
in-demand data skills. LangSmith sells observability and online/offline evaluation capabilities.
Those are category-demand signals, not proof that this pack or price will sell.

- Upwork demand report: <https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai>
- LangSmith pricing/evaluation: <https://www.langchain.com/pricing>

## Sequence

1. Publish and independently verify the free ten-case starter.
2. Use the repository's nonbinding, no-payment interest issue template with clear AI-assistance
   disclosure and a warning against private data.
3. Test at most two relevant, rules-compliant channels at a time: a useful GitHub project page and
   individually tailored replies to active buyer requests. No scraped or bulk outreach.
4. Record qualified views, inbound interest, explicit buy/commission requests, orders, paid funds,
   cleared funds, refunds, and support burden as separate events.
5. Do not create a checkout until the first-sale gate passes. A later Gumroad test is attractive
   because it has no monthly fee and acts as merchant of record, but identity/KYC and exact terms
   remain a human checkpoint.

## Decision gate

A qualified view is a view of a unique, unlisted channel-entry Markdown path in GitHub Traffic's
Popular content table during that path's declared activation window. Create a separate path before
using each external channel link. At least once every 14 days, append the UTC window, path, channel,
raw views, and manually logged owner previews to the validation log; subtract those previews.
Direct README/repository traffic, aggregate repository views, referrer totals, CI, and paths with
no retained record do not count. The metric is aggregate: it does not prove identity, intent,
uniqueness, or complete bot exclusion, so the separate human-interest gate remains mandatory.

GitHub documents that repository Traffic retains visitors, referring sites, and popular content
for the previous 14 days and updates popular content daily:
<https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/viewing-activity-and-data-for-your-repository/viewing-traffic-to-a-repository>.

At 45 calendar days or 100 qualified views, whichever comes first, require at least three
qualifying self-reported interest issues and one selection of the nonbinding option requesting a
future private route to discuss commissioning the ten-case pilot. Count only an in-window public
form issue from a non-owner, non-bot account with the boundary acknowledgement and a generic
in-scope outcome; count at most one signal per GitHub account per channel. These statements are not
identity-verified. If the gate is not met, change the buyer, outcome, price, or channel before
building a larger product. No result is backfilled from duplicates, owner actions, reactions, bot
traffic, ambiguous engagement, or private messages that cannot be retained lawfully.
