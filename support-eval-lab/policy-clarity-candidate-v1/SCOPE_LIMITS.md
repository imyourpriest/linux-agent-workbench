# Scope and limits

Included: six fixed synthetic draft files, one deterministic stored ZIP, consistency fingerprints,
an exact predecessor mapping, and a future measurement contract.

Excluded: real or private input, identity/authority verification, policy currentness, permission,
legal advice, copyright clearance, AI-use detection, compliance/certification, enforcement,
production access, contact, analytics, listing, release, payment, checkout, account setup, or sale.
Hashes are consistency fingerprints only, not signatures or external trust anchors.
